Wellbeing Garden is my graduation project in digital media directing. I came up with the idea and was responsible for game design, narrative, and code. I also put together the team: two artists, a producer/manager, a composer, a sound engineer, and a writer.

The concept is a gardening sim with visual novel elements, in which the heroine has found a way into her own subconscious.

We're close to a vertical slice, but the project is on hold until November while the team is off “earning a living.”\
